CourtSense turns a tennis coach's ordinary training video into distance, speed, court position and a heatmap for each player. Meta SAM 3.1 tracks the players, and a Muse Spark agent answers the coach and checks every number it gives.

▶ Watch the 3-minute demo View the code

The problem

Coaches film every practice. Then they can only watch it.

Knowing how far a player ran, how fast, and where they stood usually takes tracking systems that most clubs can't afford. Our coach has one camera behind the baseline. CourtSense works with exactly that.

How it works

From the coach's video to the court in meters

  1. 1

    Upload the session

    The coach picks the part of the video to analyze. One streamed SAM 3.1 request finds and tracks both players through every frame. A 12-minute session is 10,688 frames and costs about $2.14.

  2. 2

    Click four court points

    Four clicks where the singles lines cross fix a homography. From then on every footstep is a position on the court in meters, measured from the center of the near baseline (the singles court is 8.23 m wide and 23.77 m long).

  3. 3

    Read the numbers

    Distance, average and peak speed, time behind the baseline, at the baseline, mid-court and at the net, the left/center/right split, and a heatmap for each player. When a measurement is rough, the value is marked with ~ instead of looking exact, and zone shares come with a range.

  4. 4

    Ask the coach assistant

    A Muse Spark agent picks its own tools, jumps the video to the moment it talks about, and can watch that clip itself. The coach can type the question or say it. Every number in its answer is checked against the data before the coach sees it, and so is every number in the coach report.

  5. 5

    Get the report

    After each session Muse Spark writes a short report from measured numbers only, in English or Turkish, our coach's language.

Meta AI inside

What each Meta model does here

Meta model or packageWhat it does in CourtSense
SAM 3.1Finds and tracks both players in every frame of a session from the text prompt "tennis player", in one streamed request through the Meta Model API.
Muse Spark 1.3Runs the coach assistant: picks its tools, watches short clips with video input, answers in English or Turkish. Also writes the coach report.
Muse Voice TranscribeLets the coach ask the assistant by voice, in English or Turkish. The words land in the question box to check before asking.

@meta-sam/react, @meta-sam/graphicsPlay the session in the browser with each player's mask drawn in their color.

Honest limits

What it doesn't do yet

  • No ball tracking

    In our footage SAM 3.1 confused ceiling lights and signs with the ball, so there are no shot or score statistics yet.

  • Rough far-side depth

    With a low camera the far player's forward and back position is rough: about 1 m of error on hand-marked frames. The app marks it and gives zone ranges instead of guessing. Left and right stay reliable.

  • Camera drift

    Slow drift of the camera's gimbal over a long session is not compensated yet.
